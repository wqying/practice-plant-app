# Title
PlantsVSHumans

## Problem
Roommates who care for the same plants find it hard to keep track of duties such as watering the plants and the amount of sunshine required. Scheduling is hard because people don't manually update the work they've done for the plants accurately.

## Requirements
These are the main goals that the PlantsVSHumans application should focus on.

Goal 1: Track watering activities
- Record watering events
- Identify upcoming and overdue watering

Goal 2: Avoid duplicate or missed care activities

Functional requirements:
- A dashboard that shows all activities throughout the week
- The activities should be categorized clearly
